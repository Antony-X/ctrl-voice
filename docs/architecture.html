ReSpeak · how it works

Your recording is never changed. Every edit is a time range.

ReSpeak keeps the original audio untouched and builds a list of edits on top of it: cut this stretch, replace that word with a new one in your voice. When you play or send, the list is rendered into a new file. That one idea is why every edit can be undone and why most of the message stays 100% your real voice.
